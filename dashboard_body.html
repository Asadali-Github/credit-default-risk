Credit risk · 30,000 accounts · UCI

A credit model that is 78% accurate can be worth nothing.

Twenty-two percent of these accounts defaulted. Predict that nobody defaults and you are right 77.9% of the time — and you have built nothing. What follows is the sequence that turns a score into a decision: pick a metric that can be failed, beat a baseline worth beating, refuse the columns you should not price on, and then choose the cut-off by what the two mistakes actually cost.
